FAQs.

Do I need voiceover experience?

Nope! Voice Playground is beginner-friendly. You don’t need any voiceover experience to join.

Do I need a “good” voice?

Not at all. You’re here to explore the voice you already have.

Will I have to participate?

Yes! This is a playground, not a webinar. Expect to read, experiment, get direction, and play. You don’t have to get anything “right.”

What do I need?

A laptop or tablet, earphones, stable internet, and a space where you’re comfortable speaking out loud. And yes, come ready to participate!

Is this a professional voiceover course?

Not exactly. We’ll play with scripts and exercises you might encounter in voiceover, but this isn’t a step-by-step course on becoming a professional voice actor. You’ll get to practice, get direction, and explore. We’ll also have time for your voiceover questions at the end.

Will the session be recorded? Do I get a replay?

Yes, I may record the session. A full replay isn’t included because Voice Playground is designed to be experienced live. Short clips may also be used for Voice Playground content and social media, with your permission.

What if I can’t make it?

Because your payment reserves one of only six spots, payments are non-refundable. Please make sure you can attend before booking your spot.
