What happens in the playground?

Come in. We’re going to play.

For three hours, we experiment with your voice by actually using it.

Warm up Get out of your head, loosen up, and get comfortable making sound.
Read Try different kinds of scripts—from stories and commercials to characters, creator reads, and more.
Play Change the intention. The emotion. The pace. The energy. Same words. Completely different read.
Get directed Try a take, get direction, then try it again and notice what changes.
Discover Notice what comes naturally, what surprises you, and what else your voice might be capable of.

There’s no one “correct” voice we’re trying to find. We’re giving you more ways to use the one you already have.

What do you leave with?

More ways to use your voice.

More confidence to experiment.

More freedom to make different choices.

More awareness of what your voice can do.

And most importantly—the experience of actually trying.
